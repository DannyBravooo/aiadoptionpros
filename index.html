01

Missed Calls & AI Reception

Give callers a useful next step when your team cannot answer.

Overflow calls, after-hours inquiries, and clear human handoffs.

Explore capabilities+

  • Missed-inquiry follow-up

    Capture an eligible missed inquiry, create a callback task, and notify the responsible person. Add service-related text responses where permissions and provider requirements support them.

  • AI phone answering

    Explore an assistant that handles approved FAQs, gathers caller details, and routes requests. Define operating hours, escalation rules, and what the assistant must not answer.

  • After-hours voice workflows

    Collect non-emergency requests outside business hours and send a structured summary to your team. Booking is included only when the calendar integration has been scoped and tested.

Best first step: review your current phone system and callback ownership.

Planning ranges

Missed-call workflow: $500–$1,500 setup + $100–$300/month. Phone answering: $1,000–$3,000 setup + $200–$600/month. After-hours voice: $1,000–$3,000 setup + $250–$600/month.

Website Chat & Lead Intake

Turn website questions into organized, actionable inquiries.

Website conversations, intake forms, and lead routing.

Explore capabilities+

  • Website chat to booking

    Answer approved questions, collect contact details, and guide visitors toward a request or a supported scheduling flow. Make it clear when an appointment is requested versus confirmed.

  • Structured intake

    Ask the questions your team needs before responding: service requested, location, availability, and relevant project details. Collect only what is needed for the agreed purpose.

  • Lead qualification & routing

    Apply your approved service-area and job-fit rules, route inquiries to the right person, and flag exceptions for human review rather than silently discarding them.

Best first step: map the questions your team asks before accepting a job.

Planning ranges

Website chat: $500–$2,000 setup + $100–$300/month. Intake and qualification: $1,000–$2,500 setup + $200–$500/month.

Keep appointments and open estimates from falling through the cracks.

Reminders, scheduling handoffs, and timely estimate follow-up.

Reviews & Customer Re-engagement

Stay connected after the job and make it easier for customers to return.

Review requests, response drafts, and permission-based outreach.

Explore capabilities+

  • Consistent review requests

    Request honest feedback after completed work using agreed eligibility rules. Avoid incentives tied to positive reviews or filtering customers based on sentiment.

    Prepare response drafts based on approved tone and escalation rules. Keep sensitive, disputed, or personal matters under human review.

    Assess list quality, contact permissions, suppression records, and campaign purpose before reconnecting with past customers. Define attribution before any performance-based pricing.

Best first step: review your post-job process and contact-list permissions.

Restaurant Ordering & Reservations

Explore a clearer path from phone inquiry to order or reservation.

Phone workflows, menu logic, and integration feasibility.

A little clarity

Questions before the next step.

Do I need to know which service I want?

No. Start with the problem in your current process. We will help determine whether a simple workflow, a deeper assessment, or no new automation is the appropriate next step.

Do I need the $1,500 audit first?

Not for every project. A simple, clearly scoped workflow may go straight to a proposal. The audit is available when a broader assessment would be useful.

Can you work with our existing systems?

We assess your provider, account permissions, available features, and the exact handoff before committing. No integration is assumed supported without a feasibility check.

How quickly can we launch?

Timing depends on access, provider approvals, integration requirements, and testing. A target timeline is agreed after the dependencies and scope are understood.

Can you guarantee bookings or revenue?

No. We can agree on functional acceptance tests and review activity, completed work, costs, and failures. Customer demand and business outcomes are not guaranteed.

Do you require a long-term contract?

Implementation is scoped separately. Ongoing support is month-to-month with 30 days’ notice, under the terms agreed in your proposal.

What about privacy and customer messaging?

Messaging permissions, data access, retention, vendor requirements, and any relevant disclosures need to be reviewed for the actual workflow before activation. Sensitive or consequential actions require appropriate human oversight.

What is the difference between the chatbot and a confirmed booking?

The assistant on this page collects a callback or booking request and sends it to the team. Your exact appointment time is confirmed separately; it does not show live calendar availability.

Do you support restaurant POS systems?

We assess the specific POS provider, integration access, menu structure, and supported behavior first. Ordering, menu setup, and reservation workflows are quoted only after feasibility review.
